TRANSFORM-6 for macOS - please read
===================================

This is an UNSIGNED test build (no paid Apple Developer certificate yet), so
macOS Gatekeeper will complain the first time. That's expected. Two steps:

1) INSTALL
   Double-click "TRANSFORM-6.pkg". If macOS says it's from an unidentified
   developer, either:
     - right-click (or Control-click) the .pkg and choose "Open", then
       "Open" again, OR
     - go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway".

   The installer puts:
     - the VST3 in  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
     - the AU in    /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (for Logic/GarageBand)
     - the app + engine in  /Applications/TRANSFORM-6

2) LET IT RUN (clears the "quarantine" flag on the unsigned files)
   Open Terminal and paste this one line, then press Return:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/TRANSFORM-6" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/TRANSFORM-6.vst3" "/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/TRANSFORM-6.component"

   Without this, the app may refuse to launch its engine, and your DAW may
   skip the plugin while scanning.

USING IT
   - Standalone app: /Applications/TRANSFORM-6/TRANSFORM-6.app
   - To RECORD from an audio interface: open the app, allow microphone access
     when asked, then in the app's audio settings pick your interface as the
     input. If a recording comes out silent, make sure "Mute audio input" is
     OFF in those settings.
   - In a DAW: rescan plugins. VST3 shows under Pathfinder Pressings; AU shows
     as TRANSFORM-6.

This build is universal - it runs natively on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
